Shark Week

  1. Yell "Shark Week!" and finish someone's unattended bottomless drink.
  2. A drink is fair game after 1 minute without a sip.
  3. It's safe if the owner is touching it or has said they're leaving the table.
  4. Bottomless only. After bottomless ends, it doesn't count.
  5. An illegal Shark Week scores 0, and the victim keeps their drink point.
  6. Collusion is illegal. If in doubt, ask Chad.

After brunch

  1. Brunch ends at midnight, or when fewer than 3 people are still together at a bar.
  2. Events like birthdays or a volo player party are exceptions.
  3. The playoff finale is worth more points.
